Solar details

How a solar system works

01

Solar Panels Collect

Panels convert sunlight into direct current (DC) electricity during the day.

02

Inverter Converts

The inverter changes DC power into alternating current (AC) used by your appliances.

03

Home Uses Power

Your property uses solar power first, and extra electricity can charge a battery or go to the grid.

Before and after solar

See the difference

Before Solar

  • Complete dependence on grid electricity
  • Monthly bills affected by tariff increases
  • Grid power may be unavailable during outages
  • Higher indirect carbon impact

After Solar

  • On-site renewable energy generation
  • Lower daytime electricity costs
  • Battery backup can support essential loads
  • Clean power with a smaller carbon footprint
